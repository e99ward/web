I was born in 1978 and grew up in the South Korean countryside. During my school years, I spent my afternoons helping out on the farm after class. Everything changed when I went to high school and moved into a dormitory where studying was the top priority. That intense focus paid off—it led me to the top university in Korea.

I majored in Materials Science, focusing primarily on abnormal grain growth in ceramics—a key topic within sintering. In this field, understanding how microstructures relate to material properties is everything. This expertise naturally paved the way for my current role, where I continue to research materials every day.

I also have a deep passion for mathematics. If money were no object, I`d love to fully immerse myself in mathematical theory. Realistically, though, that's a distant dream! So instead, I channel that passion into coding as a hobby. Learning languages like C++, Python, and JavaScript has been an absolute blast.

I love discovering and learning new things, which is why traveling is my absolute favorite pastime. While relaxing is great, the real joy comes from experiencing new cultures and worlds. I`ve traveled to many countries, including the US, Japan, China, Belgium, Spain, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, and Germany. Spending a sabbatical year in the United States was, without a doubt, an unforgettable highlight of my life.
