Model equations, assumptions & limitations

Illustrative, not calibrated. Defaults roughly reproduce the 40 Hz, open-room flow using an assumed fan curve and an arbitrary allocation of resistance. This is not validation against your probe pressures.

Fan curve: H = H₀(f/60)² − (H₀/Q₀²)Q². Speed is assumed proportional to frequency. The stated 120 m³/min and 130 mmAq are treated as separate reference endpoints at an assumed 60 Hz, not as one duty point. All fan curves and losses use total pressure consistently.

Resistance: Δp = Δp₃₀(Q/30)². The five-inlet/housing loss and filter loss use inlet flow Qᵢ. Shared pipe and outlet losses use process flow Q = Qᵢ + Qₗ. The leak bypasses the complete upstream filter branch: Qₗ = (leak flow at 100 Pa)√(Δp/100).

Process balance: H(Q,f) = Δp_entry + Δp_filter + Δp_pipe + Δp_outlet − P_room. Room balance: Q_supply = Q_process + Q_main-exhaust. Outside gauge pressure is zero. No outside ingress, constant density, negligible room velocity, and no transient behavior are assumed.

Main exhaust: Uses a separate quadratic fan curve and duct loss. Automatic mode adjusts frequency between 0 and the chosen maximum. If the target is unreachable, the solver calculates actual room pressure at the limiting fan speed. Pressure-study mode instead imposes pressure and reports the required exhaust, without applying the fan’s speed limit.

No reverse flow: An ideal non-return device is assumed on each exhaust path. An unpowered fan may pass forward flow from a positively pressurized room. Points beyond a fan’s free-delivery range are flagged as outside the assumed fan curve’s reliable range.

Pressure budget: Values are modeled total gauge pressures at lumped nodes, not static readings at probes 1–5. Shared pipe resistance includes reducers, expansion, fittings, and baseline discharge loss. Its spatial distribution is not resolved. Electrical power = QH/η, with Q converted to m³/s; motor idle losses and efficiency variation are omitted.

Measured inlet flow at 40 Hz is 28.10 m³/min; outlet estimate is 28.86 m³/min, both at approximately zero room pressure. Use the measured layout to inspect original readings. General fan/system-curve background: DOE fan system sourcebook.
